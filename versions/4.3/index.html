Create Your Account

Your name is saved on the big server forever. Nobody else can ever use it, and your items and spot are kept under it. You can log in from any computer with your password.

Names: letters, numbers, spaces, _ and - (up to 16). Passwords: at least 6 characters. Don't use a password from another website, and remember it - it can't be reset.
